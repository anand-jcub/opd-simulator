How it works

Current: every patient walks in, stands in line at Registration (2 counters), then in a single line at their department's Vitals desk, then stands outside the consultation room (2 doctors per department), then queues at Pharmacy (2 counters).

Proposed: the chosen share of patients book a time slot (07:40–11:30) and go straight to Vitals; walk-ins still register. Each Vitals desk keeps two lines (appointment / walk-in) served alternately and issues a token number. Patients then wait seated and are called to consultation in token order.

Both systems use the identical patient cohort (same random seed, departments and service times) and identical staffing; only arrival pattern, routing and queue discipline differ. "Standing time" counts time in standing queues; seated token waiting is reported separately. Friction index = weighted standing time + seated time + peak crowd penalties (0–99).
